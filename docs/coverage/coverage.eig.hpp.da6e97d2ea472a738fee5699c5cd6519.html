76 /**
77 * @brief Unpack eigenvectors of real matrices from nda::lapack::geev or nda::lapack::ggev output.
78 *
79 * @details For real matrices, nda::lapack::geev and nda::lapack::ggev store the left and right eigenvectors in
80 * packed format in the columns \f$ \mathbf{v}^{(L)}_j \f$ and \f$ \mathbf{v}^{(R)}_j \f$ of real matrices
81 * \f$ \mathbf{V}_L \f$ and \f$ \mathbf{V}_R \f$, respectively.
82 *
83 * The unpacking uses the imaginary parts of the eigenvalues (\f$ \mathbf{w}^{(i)} \f$ for `geev` or \f$
84 * \boldsymbol{\alpha}^{(i)} \f$ for `ggev`) to determine whether eigenvalues are real or form complex conjugate
85 * pairs:
86 * - If the eigenvalue \f$ \lambda_j \f$ is real, i.e. if the imaginary part is zero, then the corresponding
87 * - left eigenvector is given by \f$ \mathbf{u}_j = \mathbf{v}^{(L)}_j \f$.
88 * - right eigenvector is given by \f$ \mathbf{v}_j = \mathbf{v}^{(R)}_j \f$.
89 * - If the eigenvalues \f$ \lambda_j \f$ and \f$ \lambda_{j + 1} \f$ form a complex conjugate pair, i.e. if the
90 * imaginary part is positive, then the two corresponding
91 * - left eigenvectors are given by \f$ \mathbf{u}_j = \mathbf{v}^{(L)}_j + i \mathbf{v}^{(L)}_{j+1} \f$ and \f$
92 * \mathbf{u}_{j+1} = \mathbf{v}^{(L)}_j - i \mathbf{v}^{(L)}_{j+1} \f$.
93 * - right eigenvectors are given by \f$ \mathbf{v}_j = \mathbf{v}^{(R)}_j + i \mathbf{v}^{(R)}_{j+1} \f$ and \f$
94 * \mathbf{v}_{j+1} = \mathbf{v}^{(R)}_j - i \mathbf{v}^{(R)}_{j+1} \f$.
95 *
96 * The resulting matrix is always returned in nda::F_layout.
97 *
98 * @note All input arrays are required to satisfy nda::mem::have_host_compatible_addr_space and to have the same real
