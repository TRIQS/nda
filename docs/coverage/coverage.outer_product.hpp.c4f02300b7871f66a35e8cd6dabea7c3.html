28 * @details It calculates the outer product \f$ \mathbf{C} = \mathbf{A} \otimes \mathbf{B} \f$, such that
29 * \f[
30 * \mathbf{C}_{i_1 \ldots i_k j_1 \ldots j_l} = \mathbf{A}_{i_1 \ldots i_k} \mathbf{B}_{j_1 \ldots j_l} \; .
31 * \f]
32 * Here, \f$ \mathbf{A} \f$ and \f$ \mathbf{B} \f$ are the input arrays with shape \f$ (m_1, \ldots, m_k) \f$ and \f$
33 * (n_1, \ldots, n_l) \f$, respectively. The resulting array \f$ \mathbf{C} \f$ has shape \f$ (m_1, \ldots, m_k, n_1,
34 * \ldots, n_l) \f$.
35 *
36 * The outer product is performed by calling nda::blas::ger, which imposes various constraints on the supported input
37 * arrays/views, e.g.
38 * - their memory layouts have to be the same and either nda::C_layout or nda::F_layout,
39 * - they have to be contiguous in memory,
40 * - etc.
41 *
42 * See nda::blas::ger for more details.
43 *
44 * @tparam A nda::MemoryArray type.
45 * @tparam B nda::MemoryArray type.
